      "Form" compares your last 5 games on a hero with the ones before them.

      How this works
      • Form scores each game against your other games on the same hero, using your share of the lobby's souls, damage, kills + assists and deaths. 0 is your usual; +1 is a clearly better game than usual. A hero needs 5 games before its games get scored.
      • Earned win: won and played above your usual. Passenger win: won, but below your usual. Unlucky loss: played above your usual and lost anyway. Rough game: lost and played below your usual.
      • Tags (Farmer, Damage, Playmaker, Survivor, Support) mean you placed top 3 (top 2 for Survivor and Support) in that one lobby. They're for bragging and don't change the verdict.
      • The verdict combines rank points, form and consistency (how many rough games you had), each compared with the window before. Climbing puts you in harder lobbies, so if your lobbies got 3+ subranks tougher, placing lower there isn't counted against you.
      • Ranked / All games: "All games" adds your unranked matches (since the ranked season began) to form, consistency, the game list and heroes. Rank points only ever come from ranked games, and the rank chart stays ranked-only.
      • It never guesses why a game went the way it did. For that, use POSTMORTEM.

      Data from deadlock-api.com. New games can take a while to show up there.
